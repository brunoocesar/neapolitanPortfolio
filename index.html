A BIT ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm
Neapolitan.

I've been building for Roblox games for over three years. Environments and assets are what I enjoy most.

I also make 3D models, thumbnails and logos. When a project needs an extra hand, I can help with animations and basic game engineering too.

I keep this portfolio focused on the projects that best show what I do. I care about working with good people and making the team a positive place to be. Creating games has been a dream of mine since I was a kid, and it still feels just as exciting.
