Hi, I’m
Neapolitan.

I’m a Roblox builder specializing in environments and assets, with over three years of experience working on games.

Building is my main focus, but I also work with 3D modeling, thumbnails, logos, animations, and basic game engineering when a project needs extra support.

This portfolio brings together my main and most relevant projects. I value a positive work environment and see game development as more than a job. It’s a long-time passion that started when I was a kid.
